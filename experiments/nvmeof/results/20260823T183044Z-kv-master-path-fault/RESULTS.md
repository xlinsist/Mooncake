# KV Master-path fault evidence

## Outcome

The planned six-trial matrix stopped after its first direct trial because the
strict post-client-close gate found a product failure. The persistent Store
client recovered after the three-second client-to-Master TCP drop and completed
eight post-fault transactions, but its timed-out put remained unremovable for
15 seconds. Closing that client and opening a fresh client did not clear the
residue within a second independent 15-second window.

This is a failed matrix with one valid completed trial, not a partial pass. The
remaining five trials were intentionally not run after the stop condition.

## Current trial

- Run: `20260823T183044Z`, direct, 128 KiB, configured concurrency 1.
- Pre/fault/post: 9 successful transactions, 1 failed fault-window put, and 8
  successful post-fault transactions.
- First failure detection: `1023.492 ms` after fault insertion.
- Persistent-client recovery: `145.892 ms` after fault removal.
- Safety: the failed put was never readable and published zero COMPLETE
  replicas in both audit windows.
- Lifecycle failure: all 59 initial and 59 fresh-client audit samples returned
  `remove=-703` (`REPLICA_IS_NOT_READY`); observed spans were `14.491 s` and
  `14.505 s`, followed by explicit deadline probes with the same result.
- Fault proof: the exact OUTPUT DROP rule counted 35 packets / 4,999 bytes and
  was absent afterward.

## Recovery and continuity

The controller's EXIT path restored a root-owned `round_robin` Master. The
final 12-object smoke verified and removed all objects, placed 6 on local NVMe
and 6 on remote NoF, and observed zero phantom replicas. Final client OUTPUT
policy was exactly `-P OUTPUT ACCEPT`.

The target service snapshot before the run and the explicitly captured
post-abort snapshot are byte-identical (`MainPID=2072748`, active/running).
The corresponding subsystem JSON is also byte-identical and retains
`nqn.2026-08.local.mooncake:nof-phase1`, `10.0.0.5:4420`, NSID 1.

## Claim boundary

This evidence covers one sequential client, a reversible client-to-Master TCP
fault, and 128 KiB remote KV operations. It demonstrates fast operation-path
recovery and safe non-publication, while exposing an unresolved failed-put
lifecycle residue. It does not test target failure, NoF data-path failure,
Master HA, serving metrics, or true concurrency.

`trial-summary.csv`, `audit-summary.json`, and `matrix-conclusion.json` are
generated by `aggregate.py`. The three archives preserve every pilot and the
final stopped run without rewriting their raw evidence.
